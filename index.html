🌲 Visitor Guide

Planning Your Trip: Yosemite Valley is accessible year-round via Highway 140. Tioga Road (Hwy 120) closes in winter. Always carry chains Nov-April.

Best Times: May-June for waterfalls, Sept-Oct for hiking. Summer requires reservations.
